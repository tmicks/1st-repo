# Website Structure & Page Copy

Build this as the Kajabi **Site** (not a product) — public marketing pages, with `Client Portal` gating into the members-only area. Nav order below is the final nav order.

```
HOME | ABOUT | SERVICES | PROGRAMS | CLIENT PORTAL | SUCCESS STORIES | RESOURCE CENTER | BOOK CONSULTATION | CONTACT
```

CTAs in the nav bar (top-right, sticky): **Apply Today** (secondary button) and **Book Consultation** (primary button).

## SERVICES

One page, three anchored sections matching the Tiers — this is the "what you get" page, `/programs` is the "how the curriculum is structured" page.

### Financial Stability
Personal financial assessment, budget building, credit report review and action planning, banking alignment (deposit consistency, cash flow, documentation) — everything needed to qualify for what comes next.

### Housing Access
Rent-ready documentation and application packet support, housing stability coaching, and a homeownership pathway (mortgage basics, down payment planning, closing costs).

### Entrepreneur Pathway
Business entity formation (LLC, EIN, operating agreement), business banking and recordkeeping, business credit and capital pathways, and — for construction/clean-energy participants — the Illinois Shines EEP → EEC pipeline and EEC Incubator.

Each section ends with a `Learn More → /programs#[tier]` and `Apply Today` button.

## PROGRAMS

This is the curriculum page — maps directly to the Kajabi Program Library in `02-kajabi-curriculum.md`. For each Product/Tier, show: what's inside (modules), what you walk away with (certificate), and estimated duration.

- **Economic Stability Service** (orientation product) — Welcome, Orientation, Program Expectations, Forms, Assessment
- **Tier 1 — Financial Stability, Credit & Banking** — 4 modules → Financial Stability Certificate
- **Tier 2 — Housing Access & Stability** — 3 modules → Housing Access Certificate
- **Tier 3 — Entrepreneur & Economic Participation** — 5 modules → Entrepreneur & Economic Participation Certificate
- Completing all three tiers → **Economic Stability Certificate** (Program Completion)

Include a visual progress path graphic: Enrollment → Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Tier 3 → Program Completion Certificate.

## CLIENT PORTAL

This nav item is **not a marketing page** — it's the login gateway. Logged-out visitors see a simple "Log In" card with a link to `/apply` for non-participants. Logged-in participants land directly on the Client Dashboard (`03-client-dashboard.md`), inside the Kajabi members area.
