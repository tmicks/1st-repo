# Kajabi Program Library — Full Curriculum Build

Build as **4 Kajabi Products** (Content Categories inside each = Modules; Posts inside each Category = Lessons). Every lesson follows the same downloads template so participants know what to expect and staff know what to produce.

**Lesson download template (every lesson, no exceptions):**
`PDF summary · Worksheet · Checklist · Template · Worked example · Action Plan`

---

## Product 1 — Economic Stability Service (Orientation)

*Purpose: onboard, set expectations, establish baseline before Tier 1 unlocks.*

- **Welcome** — video from program director, what the Institute is, how the three tracks fit together
- **Orientation** — how Kajabi works, how coaching works, how to reach your coach, community guidelines
- **Program Expectations** — attendance/engagement expectations, milestone structure, certificate requirements, what happens if you fall behind
- **Forms** — intake forms, consent/release, funding-source paperwork (embedded or linked to Airtable-fed forms — see `07-integration-architecture.md`)
- **Assessment** — Beginning Assessment (see `09-certificates-and-assessments.md`) — gates access to Tier 1 Module 1

## Product 2 — Tier 1: Financial Stability, Credit & Banking

**Module 1 — Financial Assessment**
| Lesson | Objective |
|---|---|
| Personal Financial Snapshot | Establish current-state baseline: income, debt, savings, obligations |
| Budget Builder | Build a working monthly budget using the snapshot |
| Income Analysis | Identify all income sources, stability, and gaps |
| Expense Analysis | Categorize fixed vs. variable expense, identify reduction targets |

**Module 2 — Credit Support**
| Lesson | Objective |
|---|---|
| Understanding Credit | What a credit score is, how it's calculated, why it matters for housing/business |
| Credit Factors | The 5 factors (payment history, utilization, length, mix, inquiries) |
| Reviewing Credit Reports | How to pull and read all 3 bureau reports |
| Understanding Derogatory Accounts | Collections, charge-offs, judgments — what they mean and options |
| Understanding Utilization | How utilization is calculated and target ratios |
| Credit Action Plan | Participant builds their own 90-day credit action plan (hands off to coach/Credit Repair Cloud casework if disputes are needed) |

**Module 3 — Banking Alignment**
| Lesson | Objective |
|---|---|
| Why Banking Matters | Banking relationship as a prerequisite for housing and business lending |
| PNC Banking Model | Program's banking partner model — account features, how it supports the pathway |
| Deposit Consistency | Building a regular deposit pattern lenders/landlords look for |
| Cash Flow Management | Managing timing of income vs. obligations |
| Documentation | What banking documentation will be needed in Tier 2/3 |

**Module 4 — Financial Stability Plan**
| Lesson | Objective |
|---|---|
| Budget | Finalized working budget |
| Savings | Savings target-setting (emergency fund, housing/business goal) |
| Financial Calendar | Bill/deposit/review calendar |
| Financial Milestones | Defines the milestones tracked in Airtable and shown on the dashboard |

**Gate:** Completing Module 4 + coach sign-off → **Financial Stability Certificate** issued, Tier 2 unlocks.

## Product 3 — Tier 2: Housing Access & Stability

**Module 1 — Rent Ready**
Documentation · Application Packet · Income Verification · Rental Budget

**Module 2 — Housing Stability**
Maintaining Housing · Financial Planning · Lease Education

**Module 3 — Homeownership**
Mortgage Basics · Down Payment Planning · Closing Costs · Homeownership Pathway

**Gate:** Completing Module 3 + housing coach sign-off → **Housing Access Certificate** issued, Tier 3 unlocks.

## Product 4 — Tier 3: Entrepreneur & Economic Participation

**Module 1 — Business Formation**
Choosing an Entity · LLC · EIN · Operating Agreement · Business Checklist

**Module 2 — Business Banking**
Business Accounts · Recordkeeping · Accounting Basics · Cash Flow

**Module 3 — Business Capital Pathways**
Understanding Business Credit · Vendor Accounts · Financing Basics · Responsible Capital Management · Capital Planning

**Module 4 — Illinois Shines**
Overview · EEP (Equity Eligible Person) · EEC (Equity Eligible Contractor) · Contractor Participation · Project Pipeline

**Module 5 — EEC Incubator**
What is an Incubator · Business Capacity · Mentorship · Project Readiness · Scaling

**Gate:** Completing all 5 modules + business coach sign-off → **Entrepreneur & Economic Participation Certificate** issued.

**Program Completion:** All three Tier certificates earned → **Economic Stability Certificate** (top-level program completion credential).
