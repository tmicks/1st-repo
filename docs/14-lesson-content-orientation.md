## Orientation

**OBJECTIVE:** Participant knows how Kajabi, coaching, and community work together, and how to navigate their portal.

**Key teaching points:**
- Your Client Portal is home base: Progress, Current Tier, Current Assignment, Next Coaching Call, Documents Needed, Milestones, and Certificate Progress all live on one dashboard.
- Lessons unlock in order inside each Tier — you can't skip ahead, but you can move at your own pace within what's unlocked.
- Your coach is assigned after your Beginning Assessment and is your primary point of contact — not this portal, not a general support inbox.
- Community groups are matched to your track automatically; you'll see the groups relevant to you, not the whole Institute's traffic.
- The Weekly Live Call is open to everyone; Office Hours are track-specific and bookable directly with your coach.

**Practical application (worksheet):** A short "portal scavenger hunt" — find your dashboard, your assigned community group, and the button to book your first coaching call. Confirms navigation before anything substantive starts.

**Downloads:** PDF (portal map/screenshot walkthrough) · Worksheet (scavenger hunt above) · Checklist (5 things to do before your first coaching call) · Action Plan (n/a — folds into Program Expectations lesson).

## Program Expectations

**OBJECTIVE:** Participant understands the engagement standard, milestone structure, and what happens if they fall behind.

**Key teaching points:**
- Engagement expectations: log in weekly at minimum, attend or watch the replay of your track's coaching touchpoint, respond to your coach within 3 business days.
- Milestone structure: each Tier has module-level milestones tracked in your case record; certificates require both lesson completion *and* coach sign-off — finishing the content isn't automatic completion.
- Falling behind isn't a penalty system — 7 days of inactivity triggers a check-in from your coach, not a warning. The goal is to catch a stall early, not punish it.
- Funding/grant-covered seats may carry attendance or reporting requirements tied to the funding source — your coach will tell you explicitly if this applies to you.
- Program pace is designed to be completed within [set your program's target window, e.g., 4-6 months per Tier] but coaches can adjust for documented life circumstances.

**Practical application (worksheet):** Participant signs/acknowledges a one-page expectations agreement and sets their own weekly "check-in day."

**Downloads:** PDF (expectations one-pager) · Worksheet (acknowledgment + weekly check-in day selector) · Checklist (what "falling behind" actually triggers, step by step) · Action Plan (put your chosen check-in day on your calendar now).

## Forms

**OBJECTIVE:** Participant completes all required intake/consent/funding paperwork before Tier 1 opens.

**Key teaching points:**
- Consent/release forms: program participation agreement, media/testimonial release (opt-in, not required), data-sharing consent for funder reporting.
- Funding-source paperwork: only shown/required if applicable to the participant's funding source (grant vs. self-pay) — coach or case manager confirms which forms apply.
- These forms feed directly into the participant's Airtable case record on the backend — this is the one point in the journey where "paperwork" and "your case file" are the same action, so accuracy here matters for every report generated later.
- Nothing here is meant to be a barrier — if a form is confusing or a question doesn't apply, participants are told explicitly to flag it to their coach rather than guess.

**Practical application:** Embedded forms (or linked forms feeding the Airtable intake automation per `07-integration-architecture.md`) — completion of all required forms is itself the gate that unlocks the Assessment lesson.

**Downloads:** Checklist (which forms are required vs. optional) only — no worksheet/template/example needed, this lesson *is* the forms.

## Assessment

**OBJECTIVE:** Participant completes the Beginning Assessment, which determines track confirmation and coach assignment.

**Key teaching points:**
- This is the same assessment referenced in `docs/09-certificates-and-assessments.md` — 7 questions covering budgeting confidence, credit report history, banking consistency, housing status, business intent, biggest barrier, and 6-month success definition.
- Two open-text questions (biggest barrier, success definition) are read personally by the assigned coach before the first call — participants are told this explicitly so they answer honestly rather than briefly.
- Submitting this assessment is the trigger that assigns a coach and unlocks Tier 1 Module 1 (see the automation flow in `docs/04-automations-and-emails.md`).
- There's no passing/failing this assessment — it's a matching tool, not a gate on ability.

**Practical application:** The assessment itself (see `09-certificates-and-assessments.md` for the exact question set) — no separate worksheet.

**Downloads:** none beyond the assessment form itself.
