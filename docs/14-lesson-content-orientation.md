## Module 1 — Welcome

### Lesson 1 — Welcome to the Economic Stability Service

> Welcome to the Economic Stability Service.
>
> This program is designed to help you build a stronger foundation for long-term economic stability and participation.
>
> Throughout this program, you will have access to education, resources, coaching, assessments, and action plans designed around your individual goals.
>
> Your journey may include areas such as:
> - Financial stability
> - Credit and banking
> - Housing access
> - Homeownership preparation
> - Entrepreneurship
> - Business development
> - Workforce and career opportunities
> - Clean energy contractor opportunities
>
> Your success starts with understanding where you are today and creating a realistic plan for where you want to go.
>
> Take your time, complete each assignment, and communicate with your support team when you need assistance.
>
> Let's get started.

### Lesson 2 — What This Program Is

> The Economic Stability Service is a structured support program designed to help participants identify financial and economic barriers and develop practical pathways toward greater stability.
>
> This is not simply a course that you watch and finish.
>
> You will be asked to learn, assess, plan, and take action.
>
> Your program may address:
>
> **Financial Stability**
> Understanding your income, expenses, budgeting, savings, credit, and banking.
>
> **Housing Access**
> Preparing for rental housing, maintaining housing stability, and developing a pathway toward homeownership when appropriate.
>
> **Entrepreneurship & Economic Participation**
> Exploring business formation, business banking, capital readiness, and opportunities to participate in economic development.
>
> **Workforce & Career Development**
> Connecting your skills, education, and career goals with opportunities for advancement.
>
> Your specific pathway will depend on your assessment, goals, eligibility, and available services.

### Lesson 3 — What You Can Expect

> As a participant, you can expect a structured process that includes:
>
> 1. **Assessment** — We begin by understanding your current situation and goals.
> 2. **Education** — You will receive information and resources relevant to your pathway.
> 3. **Action Planning** — You will develop specific goals and action steps.
> 4. **Coaching & Support** — You may receive guidance and support throughout your program.
> 5. **Progress Tracking** — Your progress and milestones will be reviewed throughout the program.
> 6. **Next Steps** — At completion, you will receive guidance on continuing your economic development journey.
>
> Your participation matters.
>
> The more consistently you complete your assignments, provide requested documentation, attend support sessions, and communicate with your team, the more effectively we can support your goals.

## Module 2 — Orientation

### Lesson 1 — Program Expectations

> The Economic Stability Service works best when you actively participate in the process.
>
> As a participant, you are expected to:
>
> 1. **Complete your lessons and assignments** — Work through the program materials and complete the activities assigned to you.
> 2. **Provide requested information and documentation** — Some services may require documents such as identification, income information, financial records, housing documents, or business information.
> 3. **Attend scheduled coaching and support sessions** — If you have a scheduled appointment, make every effort to attend or communicate with your coach if you need to reschedule.
> 4. **Communicate with your support team** — Let your team know when you have questions, encounter a barrier, or experience a change in your circumstances.
> 5. **Take action between sessions** — Your progress depends on putting what you learn into practice.
> 6. **Protect your personal information** — Only submit sensitive information through the approved channels provided by your program team.
>
> **Your Role**
> We provide education, resources, guidance, and support. You are responsible for participating in the process and completing the actions associated with your individual plan.
>
> Progress happens when education turns into action.

### Lesson 2 — How to Use Your Client Portal

> Your Kajabi client portal is your central location for accessing your Economic Stability Service materials.
>
> Inside your portal, you may find:
> - Lessons
> - Videos
> - Worksheets
> - Forms
> - Checklists
> - Assignments
> - Program announcements
> - Coaching information
> - Progress information
> - Certificates
>
> **Your Recommended Process**
>
> **Step 1 — Log in.** Access your participant portal using your account credentials.
> **Step 2 — Check your current module.** Begin with the module and lesson assigned to you.
> **Step 3 — Complete the lesson.** Review the information and complete any required activities.
> **Step 4 — Complete assignments.** Download and complete any worksheets, forms, or action plans associated with the lesson.
> **Step 5 — Check your next step.** After completing a lesson, review what you need to complete next.
>
> **Important**
> Your portal is designed to help you stay organized. Check it regularly for new materials, assignments, announcements, and program updates.
>
> If you have difficulty accessing your account or finding something, contact your program support team.

### Lesson 3 — Coaching & Support

> Education is only one part of the Economic Stability Service.
>
> Depending on your program pathway and available services, you may have access to coaching and support in areas such as:
>
> **Financial Coaching**
> Support with budgeting, financial organization, savings goals, banking, and financial readiness.
>
> **Housing Support**
> Guidance related to rental readiness, housing stability, documentation, and long-term housing goals.
>
> **Business Coaching**
> Support with business formation, business organization, banking, planning, and economic participation.
>
> **Workforce & Career Support**
> Guidance related to career development, employment opportunities, skills, and advancement.
>
> **Clean Energy & Contractor Support**
> Where applicable, participants may receive information about clean energy workforce and contractor opportunities.
>
> **How to Get the Most From Coaching**
>
> Come prepared. Before a coaching session:
> - Review your assigned lessons.
> - Complete requested assignments.
> - Gather requested documents.
> - Write down your questions.
> - Be prepared to discuss your goals.
>
> Coaching is designed to help you move from where you are today toward where you want to be.

### Lesson 4 — How Progress Works

> Your progress through the Economic Stability Service is based on participation, completed activities, assessments, and progress toward your individual goals.
>
> **Your Journey**
>
> ASSESS → Understand your current situation.
> LEARN → Complete the education and resources relevant to your pathway.
> PLAN → Create specific goals and action steps.
> ACT → Complete the actions identified in your plan.
> REVIEW → Evaluate your progress and identify remaining barriers.
> ADVANCE → Determine your next pathway and continued opportunities.
>
> **Remember**
> Everyone's pathway will look different.
>
> Your progress should be measured against your starting point, your individual goals, and the actions you complete.
>
> If you encounter a barrier, don't stop progressing — communicate with your support team. Your plan can be adjusted when circumstances change.

### Lesson 3 — How to Submit Documents

> Your program team will tell you which documents are required and how they should be submitted.
>
> **Before You Submit**
>
> 1. **Confirm the document is requested.** Only submit documents that your program team has asked you to provide.
> 2. **Make sure the document is readable.** Photos and scans should be clear enough for the information to be reviewed.
> 3. **Check that the document is complete.** Make sure required pages are included and information is not cut off.
> 4. **Use the approved submission method.** Your program team will provide instructions for submitting documents through the appropriate secure channel.
> 5. **Keep your own copy.** Whenever possible, retain a copy of documents you submit.
>
> **Protect Your Information**
> Some documents may contain sensitive personal or financial information. Do not send sensitive documents through an unapproved channel. If you're unsure where to submit a document, contact your program support team first.
>
> **After Submission**
> Your team may review the documentation and contact you if additional information is needed. Submitting a document does not necessarily mean that a service, approval, housing opportunity, financing option, or other outcome is guaranteed.
>
> When in doubt, ask before submitting.

## Module 4 — Beginning Assessment

### Lesson 1 — Financial Assessment

> The Financial Assessment helps your support team understand where you're starting from financially, so we can recommend the right pathway and connect you with the right coach.
>
> This isn't a test — there's no passing or failing. The more honest and complete your answers, the better we can support you.
>
> **What This Assessment Covers**
> - Income — your current sources of income and how stable they are
> - Expenses — your regular monthly costs and obligations
> - Savings — what you currently have set aside, if anything
> - Credit — whether you've reviewed your credit report in the past year, and any concerns you're already aware of
> - Banking — whether you currently have a checking/savings account you use consistently
>
> **Before You Begin**
> Having these on hand helps, but isn't required to start:
> ☐ A recent pay stub or income statement
> ☐ A general sense of your monthly expenses
> ☐ Any concerns about your credit you already know about
>
> **What Happens Next**
> Once you complete this assessment, your team will use your answers — together with your Housing and Entrepreneur Assessments — to recommend your starting Tier and match you with a coach.

### Lesson 2 — Housing Assessment

> The Housing Assessment helps us understand your current housing situation and what you're working toward — whether that's stable rental housing or eventually owning a home.
>
> **What This Assessment Covers**
> - Your current housing status (renting, staying with family/friends, unstable/transitional, own a home)
> - Any current housing stability concerns
> - Whether homeownership is a goal, and on what general timeline
> - What documentation you already have ready (ID, income verification, rental history)
>
> **Why This Matters**
> Housing goals connect directly to your Financial Assessment — your coach reviews both together when recommending your pathway.

### Lesson 3 — Entrepreneur Assessment

> The Entrepreneur Assessment helps us understand whether business ownership or economic participation is part of your goals — now or down the road.
>
> **What This Assessment Covers**
> - Whether you currently have a business, or an idea for one
> - What industry or type of work interests you
> - Whether your business is already formed (LLC, EIN, etc.) or still just an idea
> - Interest in construction, clean energy, or contractor opportunities — including the Illinois Shines EEP/EEC pathway
> - What capital or resources you think you'll need
>
> **Why This Matters**
> Not everyone starts in the Entrepreneur Pathway right away — but flagging interest here means your team can plan for it, even if you start in Financial Stability or Housing first.

### Lesson 4 — Economic Stability Goals

> You've completed your Financial, Housing, and Entrepreneur Assessments. This lesson brings everything together.
>
> **What Happens Here**
> 1. **Review** — your coach reviews all three assessments together, not just one at a time.
> 2. **Recommend** — based on your answers, your team recommends where you'll start: Tier 1 (Financial Stability), Tier 2 (Housing Access), or Tier 3 (Entrepreneur Pathway).
> 3. **Confirm** — you confirm your starting track and goals with your coach.
> 4. **Plan** — together, you'll set the specific goals that guide your first 30 days (covered in your Participant Action Plan, next).
>
> **In Your Own Words**
> Before your first coaching call, write down:
> - What does success look like for you in 6 months?
> - What's the single biggest barrier standing between you and that goal today?
>
> Your coach reads this before you meet, so be as specific and honest as you can.
>
> **One Program, Multiple Pathways**
> You may start in one track and move into another as your goals evolve. Nothing here locks you into a single path — it's a starting point, not a ceiling.

## Module 5 — Participant Action Plan

*Participant reaches this module after being matched to a starting Tier — it can run in parallel with the start of their Tier product, since its purpose is to lock in the plan they'll be coached against, not to gate Tier access.*

### Lesson 1 — Your Economic Stability Goals

> Now that your assessments are complete and you've met with your coach, it's time to write your goals down in one place — this becomes the plan you'll work from for the rest of the program.
>
> **What Goes Here**
> - Your primary goal, in your own words
> - The track you're starting in (Financial Stability / Housing Access / Entrepreneur Pathway)
> - Any secondary goals you flagged during your assessments
> - Your general timeline (next 3, 6, and 12 months)
>
> **Why This Matters**
> This isn't a one-time form. Your coach refers back to this page throughout the program to check whether your plan still matches your goals, and updates it with you if your circumstances change.
>
> Take your time. This should sound like you, not like a form.

### Lesson 2 — Recommended Service Track

> Based on your assessments and your conversation with your coach, you've been matched with a starting track.
>
> **Your Options**
> - Tier 1 — Financial Stability, Credit & Banking
> - Tier 2 — Housing Access & Stability
> - Tier 3 — Entrepreneur & Economic Participation
>
> **What This Means**
> Your starting track is where your next lessons, coaching, and milestones come from. It's not the only track you'll ever touch — many participants move through more than one as their goals evolve.
>
> **Confirm Your Track**
> Your coach confirms your track with you directly. Once confirmed, your next Tier product unlocks in your portal.

### Lesson 3 — Your First 30-Day Action Plan

> A goal without a plan is just a wish. This lesson turns your goals into specific steps for your first 30 days.
>
> **Build Your Plan**
> 1. Pick 2-3 concrete actions you can take in the next 30 days toward your primary goal.
> 2. Identify anything you need from your coach or program team to take those actions (documents, information, referrals).
> 3. Set a date to check in on your progress with your coach.
>
> **Keep It Realistic**
> Thirty days is short. Pick actions you can actually complete, not everything you eventually want to do. Bigger goals get broken into the next 30 days, and the 30 after that.

### Lesson 4 — Preparing for Your Coaching Session

> Your first full coaching session is where your action plan gets reviewed and your program pathway officially begins.
>
> **Before Your Session**
> ☐ Complete all Module 1-4 lessons
> ☐ Complete your Financial, Housing, and Entrepreneur Assessments
> ☐ Write your Economic Stability Goals and First 30-Day Action Plan
> ☐ Gather any documents your team has already requested
> ☐ Write down your questions
>
> **What to Expect**
> Your coach reviews your goals, confirms your track, walks through your 30-day plan, and answers your questions. This is the start of your working relationship with your coach — come ready to be honest about where you are.

## Module 6 — Program Completion & Next Steps

*Participants return to this module after finishing whichever Tier(s) their plan called for — it is the bookend to Module 1-5's intake, not part of the initial onboarding sequence.*

### Lesson 1 — Progress Review

> Whether you completed one Tier or all three, this is the point to look back at everything you've done since you started.
>
> **Review**
> - Which Tier(s) did you complete?
> - Which milestones did you hit along the way? (Your coach can pull these from your record.)
> - What's changed since your Beginning Assessment?
>
> **Reflect**
> Write a few sentences on what's different for you now compared to when you started. This isn't graded — it's for you, and it feeds directly into your Final Assessment next.

### Lesson 2 — Final Assessment

> Complete your Completion Assessment below. It covers:
> - Which certificate(s) you've earned
> - The outcome(s) you achieved (financial stability, housing, business formation, capital access, EEP→EEC advancement)
> - Whether you're willing to share your story for Success Stories
> - What you'd tell someone considering this program
> - What your next goal is
>
> This is the same information used for outcome reporting to funders — your honest answers help keep this program funded for the next person who needs it.

### Lesson 3 — Next Steps

> Finishing a Tier — or the full program — isn't the end of the road.
>
> **If You Completed One Tier**
> You can move directly into your next recommended Tier — your coach confirms which one fits your goals now.
>
> **If You Completed All Three Tiers**
> You've earned your Economic Stability Certificate. From here, you may be eligible for advanced pathways as the Institute grows — including academies in Homeownership, Entrepreneurship, Construction Careers, Clean Energy Contracting, Business Capital, and Leadership Development.
>
> **Either Way**
> Your coach talks through what's realistic and useful for you specifically — this page is an overview, not a decision.

### Lesson 4 — Continued Support

> Finishing your program doesn't mean losing access to support.
>
> **What's Still Available to You**
> - Your community group(s) stay open to you
> - Office Hours remain bookable if you need a one-off question answered
> - Program announcements and new opportunities still reach you
>
> **Staying Connected**
> If your contact information changes, let your team know so you don't lose access to future opportunities, including new academies as they launch.

### Lesson 5 — Economic Stability Certificate

> Congratulations — completing all three Tiers of the Economic Stability Service earns you the Economic Stability Certificate, the program's top-level credential.
>
> **What This Certificate Represents**
> - Financial Stability Certificate (Tier 1)
> - Housing Access Certificate (Tier 2)
> - Entrepreneur & Economic Participation Certificate (Tier 3)
> - Verified completion of the full program pathway
>
> **Where to Find It**
> Available in your Client Portal under Certificate Progress — downloadable and shareable.
>
> **What's Next**
> Talk to your coach about which advanced pathway makes sense for you next — this certificate is a milestone, not a finish line.
