# Economic Stability Service — Kajabi Buildout

## Strategic Model

The platform splits responsibilities across four systems, each playing a distinct role in the participant and staff experience:

| System | Role |
|---|---|
| **Kajabi** | The University — the client-facing learning, community, and coaching experience |
| **Airtable** | The Case Management System — the operational backend (records, grants, case notes) |
| **HubSpot** | The Admissions Office — marketing, lead capture, and sales funnel |
| **Credit Repair Cloud** | The Credit Operations Platform — credit repair service delivery |

**Core principle:** Participants should never see Airtable. They live entirely inside Kajabi.

---

## Recommendation

Based on everything built out over the past several months, this should **not** be marketed as a credit program. Instead, market it as an **Economic Stability Institute**, with the **Economic Stability Service** as the front door.

This positioning supports one platform serving:

- Workforce participants
- Apprentices
- Returning residents
- Community Development Corporation (CDC) clients
- Homebuyer participants
- Entrepreneurs
- EEPs (Eligible Entrepreneur Persons) pursuing EEC (Eligible Entrepreneur Contractor) opportunities
- Employers seeking retention and advancement support

It also creates a platform that can grow well beyond the current grant into employer contracts, workforce board partnerships, housing initiatives, and clean energy contractor development — aligning with the broader workforce ecosystem strategy and providing a scalable foundation for future programs.

- Financial Stability Academy
- Homeownership Academy
- Entrepreneur Academy
- Construction Career Academy
- Clean Energy Contractor Academy (EEP → EEC)
- Business Capital Academy
- Leadership & Executive Development Academy

Participants would begin in the Economic Stability Service and then continue into advanced pathways as their goals evolve.
