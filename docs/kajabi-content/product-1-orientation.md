## Post 1: Welcome

**Post title:** Welcome to the Economic Stability Service

**Body copy:**

> Welcome — you've taken the first step toward greater financial stability, housing security, and long-term economic independence.
>
> The Economic Stability Service is built around three tracks:
>
> - **Financial Stability** — credit, banking, and budgeting
> - **Housing Access** — rent-readiness, housing stability, and the path to homeownership
> - **Entrepreneur Pathway** — business formation, business credit, and capital access
>
> You'll move through this program in tiers, at your own pace, with a coach supporting you at every step. Each tier ends with a certificate, and completing all three earns your Economic Stability Certificate.
>
> Before you begin Tier 1, complete the four short lessons in this orientation. They'll take about 20 minutes and make sure you're set up for everything ahead.

## Post 2: Orientation

**Post title:** How This Program Works

**Body copy:**

> Here's what to expect:
>
> - **Self-paced learning.** Each tier is broken into modules and short lessons. Complete lessons in order — later modules build on earlier ones.
> - **Coaching.** You'll be assigned a coach after this orientation. You'll meet on the Weekly Live Call and can book Office Hours anytime you need one-on-one support.
> - **Assessments.** You'll complete a Beginning Assessment now, a Midpoint Assessment halfway through, and a Completion Assessment at the end. These help your coach track your progress and tailor support to you.
> - **Documents.** Some steps require documentation (ID, income verification, etc.). Your dashboard will always show what's needed next.
> - **Community.** You'll get access to a private group with other participants on the same track — a space for questions, encouragement, and shared wins.
>
> Your dashboard is your home base. Every time you log in, you'll see your current tier, current assignment, next coaching call, and what's needed from you.

**Body copy:**

> This short assessment helps your coach understand where you're starting from — there are no wrong answers. It covers your current financial snapshot, housing situation, and business/entrepreneurship interests, and takes about 10–15 minutes.
>
> Once submitted, you'll be matched with a coach and your Tier 1 content will unlock.
